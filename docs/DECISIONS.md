## Open questions

Raised in milestone summaries and not yet decided. Settle each one when the milestone it belongs to comes up.

- **Builds in generated music.** On the first live run the plan asked the build chunk to "end with 2.0 seconds of near silence" (the folded gap). Take 1's builds came out nearly silent (−26/−29 LUFS against −11 for the drops), and both takes' drops opened with one to two bars of silence. With the cue gone, new take 4's first build is as loud as its bed (−17.3 LUFS), but its second drop still has about 2 s of model-made silence before it (130.5–134.5 s, overlapping the muted gap). Inpainted builds stayed quiet: s3 at about −30 dB RMS, and s9 at −33 dB RMS until its last two bars, even with the stronger rise cue. The inpainted s11 drop starts with about 12 s at −18 dB before the full drop. Inpainting next to sparse beds with `context_adherence: high` may pull builds down; untested alternatives are `medium`, shorter builds, or a `--note`.
- **Longer songs.** The new selection targets (about 8 quotes, up to 150 s of speech) make songs of roughly 4–6 minutes, so each ElevenLabs take costs more (about $0.75–0.90 at $0.15/min). On the second talk (9 quotes, 103 s of speech) the preset's three speech slots gave passages of 17, 20 and 23 bars (35–48 s), against 8–11 bars on the first talk. Claude's refined arc used four slots (17, 20, 8, 15 bars).
- **Required quotes widen to whole sentences.** A quote that is part of a sentence brings the whole sentence. Word-level cuts are possible later if needed.
- **Drops after a gap come in late.** In all four takes, the model tends to open a drop with a silent bar and a riser, or a soft bar. Regenerating take 4's s11 three ways reproduced the same opening each time: the original, a regeneration with `high` adherence, and one with `medium` adherence plus the note "full energy from the very first beat, no silent or quiet lead-in". So this is now aligned locally (see Decisions). Take 4's first drop opens with 7 soft bars, and its breakdown plays at drop level (the mix takes it down 4 dB); both are left as generated, because that is the take you liked.
- **Inpainting keeps the music, not the samples.** The "kept" audio-reference ranges come back re-rendered: v1 and v2 of take 1 correlate only 0.15–0.28 as waveforms, but 0.96–0.99 as mel spectrograms (two different takes score 0.36–0.88). So kept sections sound the same but aren't bit-identical, and every regeneration is a full re-render.
- **Take beat phase.** Folding take 1's onset envelope on the beat puts its drops 40–110 ms behind our bar grid (ambient sections are too sparse to read). It could be the model's own timing or MP3 decoding; not corrected.
- **Inpainting billing** is not documented: estimates price a `regenerate` as the whole song (kept slices included), which may overstate it. Untested live.
- **ElevenLabs credits** can't be read per call: the account counter lags (it read 0 after take 1 and 5,092 after take 2), so cost entries stay estimates ($0.15/min).
- **MP3 timing.** Takes are decoded with ffmpeg; any encoder delay would offset the music against the speech by a few ms. Not measured.
- **Take 2's key** reads A major (the dominant of D minor); chroma-based key reading on generated music is a heuristic.
- **Mix levels by ear.** Speech +2 LU over the music, −9 dB ducking, melody layer −10 LU (−6 dB more under speech) are starting guesses. On the sample talk, the ducked bed sits 13–15 dB under the voice, and the melody layer is about 15 dB under the stub's drops, which may be inaudible over a real track.
- **Clip start feel.** Clips start on the downbeat, so the first word lands 0–300 ms later (the lead pause). A pickup offset (first word on the downbeat) is possible via `clip_offset_beats`.
- **Arc refinement** was first used live on the second talk (Opus 5.5, $0.0377, valid on the first attempt). It split the long final passage around a third build, gap and drop, so the song has three waves (5:46 instead of 4:37). Whether `--refine-arc` should be the default when the passages run long is open.
- **Test time.** `uv run pytest` takes about 150 s now (was 19 s at M3): the mix, stub and pYIN tests do real DSP on 30–70 s songs, and the ElevenLabs tests now mix too.
- **Float WAVs from demucs** (`01_clean.wav`, M1) are still written by libsndfile, whose PEAK chunk timestamps every write; re-running `isolate` changes the file's hash even when the audio is identical.
- **Flat melodies (M6).** The speaker stays within about 4 semitones, so phrases repeat notes (F F F F). Possible fix: a `melody.contour_gain` that widens pitch movement around the median before snapping.
- **Weak key detection.** Speech isn't tonal: the confidence is 0.25 on the sample talk, so the preset's minor-mode preference effectively picks between relative keys. `--key` overrides it. Revisit if the generated music clashes.
- **Melody density.** Speech has about 4–5 syllables per second, but the melody has 1–2 notes per second after 1/8-grid quantizing. Whether it still sounds like the speaker's phrase hasn't been judged by ear yet.
- **Static chords.** Notes cluster on the tonic, so chord choices lean heavily on i. This probably matters little once the generated track carries the harmony (M5).
- **Sentences without pauses.** About 5% of sentence boundaries in the sample talk have no pause, so the cut lands on speech (around −28 dBFS). The cut level is shown per clip; review could flag those clips.
- **Small ASR drops without a transcript.** Gap repair catches gaps of 3 s or more holding at least 1.5 s of speech. Smaller drops show up as "unspoken" only when an official transcript exists.
- **Paths tested only with fakes:** the validation retry, the server-side refusal fallback, and audio playback during review.
- **Cost estimates.** The input estimate (2.2 characters per token) is calibrated on one talk. The output estimate was about 3× the actual for Opus 5.5, which is conservative.
- **demucs quality** hasn't been judged by ear. Isolation left the transcript unchanged.
